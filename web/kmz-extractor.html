KML / KMZ Point Extractor

Turns KML and KMZ files into one Excel workbook.

Points become one row each, with decimal degrees, degrees–minutes–seconds, UTM and MGRS. Areas get their own sheet, with their size in m², hectares and km², and their corners listed beneath. Routes and tracks are counted but not extracted.

Drop .kml or .kmz files here, or click to choose

Your files never leave this device. The conversion runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, and there is no server to send it to. It keeps working with the network off.
